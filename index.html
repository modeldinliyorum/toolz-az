Toolz-AZ

Everyday image tools, running entirely in your browser

All utilities for compressing, resizing, converting and inspecting images. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and nothing is tracked.

The tools

Open one from the dashboard. Each is a single-purpose page.

Why it works this way

🔒

Your files stay yours

Images are read locally and drawn onto a canvas. There is no server to send them to.

⚡

Nothing to install

No account, no backend, no build step. Open the page and start working.

🎨

Light and dark

Follows your system setting and remembers your choice.
